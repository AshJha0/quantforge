## Plain idea

replay a delta hedge of one European option on a given path with a self-financing cash account; dollar-offset, regression and variance-reduction effectiveness. (Read from the inventory entry.)

Inputs (read from the inventory entry): option, quantity, rate, dividend yield, hedge volatility, a dated spot path; or two series of value changes.
