## Limits

spec 1.1.0 (KR-232, KR-233, after different-model audit A): `false_alarm_rate` is the Student-t rate of the trigger with sigma estimated from the window; the normal level is `nominal_rate`. Both assume independent normal changes, so fat tails raise the real rate; CUSUM and EWMA are out of wave 1. (Read from the inventory entry.)
