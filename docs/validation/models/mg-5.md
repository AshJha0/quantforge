## Plain idea

caplets and floorlets in Black form on the integrated abcd variance, and European swaptions by Rebonato's frozen-weight formula with exponential correlation; the terminal-measure simulation is judged against the caplets. (Read from the inventory entry.)

## Formal statement

This report claims only what the files below show: each listed golden case states inputs, an expected status and, for value-checked cases, expected outputs within the case's tolerance class (`conformance/tolerances/policy.yaml`), and the neutral runner sends every case to all four builds. Whether the four builds pass them at a given commit is recorded by the conformance workflow, not here.

Inputs (read from the inventory entry): discount curve; abcd volatility; correlation decay `beta`; period or payment times, strike, notional.
