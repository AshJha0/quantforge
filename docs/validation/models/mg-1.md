zero-coupon bond prices and European bond options under Vasicek, CIR and Hull-White, and European swaptions on Hull-White by Jamshidian, sigma calibration, Bermudan swaptions by a pinned FD scheme, caps and floors under all three models, and issuer-callable bonds; the base of the stage G Bermudan, callable-bond and exposure models. ## Limits

one factor (perfectly correlated rates); constant parameters (no time-dependent `sigma`); Vasicek and Hull-White allow negative rates without bound; no market price of risk; calibration of `sigma` only with `a` fixed (wave 2c). Bermudan swaptions and callable bonds use a first-order reference scheme, and the goldens embed its bias: measured gaps of 0.17% to 1.2% against a refined grid (review, low items; plan row R6-25). The goldens pin the scheme, not the converged price. (Read from the inventory entry.)

Use restriction for every model (inventory): research, education and conformance testing only; not for trading, valuation of record, risk limits or regulatory capital.

Limits of this report: the counts show what is checked, not that the checks are sufficient. Golden inputs are synthetic unless the case's `verified_by` says otherwise.
