## Limits

Nelson-Siegel is poorly identified, so goldens judge repricing, not parameters. No Svensson term. Robust fits depend on the outlier threshold, which is guarded in the goldens. (Read from the inventory entry.)

Use restriction for every model (inventory): research, education and conformance testing only; not for trading, valuation of record, risk limits or regulatory capital.

Limits of this report: the counts show what is checked, not that the checks are sufficient. Golden inputs are synthetic unless the case's `verified_by` says otherwise.
