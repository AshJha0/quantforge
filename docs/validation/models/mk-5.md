## Plain idea

`CN_RANNACHER_SMOOTHED`, an optional `method` on `rates.shortrate.bermudan_swaption` and `rates.shortrate.bermudan_ladder`: Crank-Nicolson with four implicit half steps after each exercise date, the curve term integrated exactly, and the exercise `max` smoothed to second order (KR-8 as revised by KR-18), with the aim that the ladder at 201/100 is within 1% of the converged model derivative. The aim is met on the three cases the method was developed on and not shown elsewhere. (Read from the inventory entry.)
