## What this release is

An independent review on 2026-11-12 found the evidence layer of v0.8.0 overstated: half the headline golden cases check no number, outside-check shares were counted generously, the mutation gate mutated nothing, and research holdout data had been downloaded and published by the recorder. It also found real model errors (the curve Jacobian, discrete barriers, expected shortfall, the FRTB total). v0.8.1 repairs both before stage J.

Plain idea: a release's numbers are only as good as the checks behind them. This one changes how the numbers are counted, and fixes the models where the review found real errors.

### Outside-check shares (R6-13, decision D6, owner decision 2026-11-12)

From `oracle/OUTSIDE_SHARES.md`, generated by `tools/outside_shares.py` and regenerated and diffed in `oracle-coverage.yml`. A case counts only as `library-object` (a library's own pricing object) or `library-primitive` (numpy or scipy numerics on a different formulation), and only when every compared output is checked. **Owner decision, 2026-11-12: the 60% floor is on value-checked cases only;** error and invariance cases are a separate evidence class. The old all-case share (error cases in the denominator) is shown beside it. Cases added late (stage F `e1_*` sweeps, stage I wave 5) are shown separately, and the floor applies without them (plan 14.10 rule 3).

**Stage F, plain idea:** where no library prices a case, a second, independent derivation of the spec's formula (written blind by the second oracle lane, not from the oracle's code) is the next-best outside check. Formal statement: a value case counts if every compared output agrees with a library object, a library primitive, or the blind re-derivation within the case tolerance; the floor holds on libraries alone plus re-derivation (84.4%) and without the 23 book cases only the spec defines (78.9%). Worked example of a disagreement: the re-derivation first disagreed on `cdsidx_f-0056` and `-0057`; the cause was the re-derivation's precision (cancellation in `1 - exp`, ruling R6-28.8), now fixed, and the goldens stand. Still disagreeing and **not counted**: `risk_f4-0087`..`0095` (9 cases), where the spec keeps the 1996 Basel plus factors by stated vintage (R6-28.1) and the re-derivation used the later FRTB table. `garch_f-0009` (`arch` stops above 1e-8, R6-28.7) and `joint_f-0004` are unverified at the 1e-8 level and not counted. Rulings R6-28.1 and R6-28.4 rest on the oracle lane's own reading of the Basel texts, with no outside check (`specifications/r6-rulings.md`, R6-28; `program/R6_HANDOVER.md`).

Stage I's 66.7% comes from a per-case register (`tools/stage_i_case_register.py`, `oracle/outside_register_i_cases.json`); no case was added to reach it, and every counted case is a numpy or scipy primitive check, not a library pricing object. Value classes with no free outside reference are named with reasons in `oracle/OUTSIDE_SHARES.md` (integer lots, beta futures, residual report, slippage, backtests).
