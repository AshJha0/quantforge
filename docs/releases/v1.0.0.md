### Who should read what

- Students: tutorials 51 to 63 and question bank set 14 ([getting started](../getting-started/README.md)); the 500 knowledge-check questions.
- Desk developers: the K-3 adapters and simulated feed codecs, K-5 lock-free snapshots, the K-6 end-of-day rehearsal and capacity limits, the cookbook's stage K recipes.
- Quants and validators: wave 2 capital operations (FRTB DRC and RRAO, IMA, CVA capital, leverage and large exposures), K-8 AAD xVA sensitivities and their blind check, the [status ladder](../validation/status-ladder.md), the [known issues](../known-issues.md) register.
- Researchers: the daily paper ledger verifier (plumbing evidence; no performance claim).

## What this release is

The stage K platform pieces in Python, Rust, Java and C++, on simulated data: the data and risk platform items of round 1, the wave 2 capital operations, infrastructure adapters, snapshots, soak and end-of-day tooling, signed release artefacts, and the stage-wide review fixes. It does not contain a tradable strategy, live orders, a persistent production store, or any claim of profitability from paper trading.
