## What this release is

The first stage K pieces, in all four builds: a bitemporal store, an incremental curve update (including an incremental path on the stage B production bootstrap), recalibration triggers, intraday risk with an error bound, a second-order Bermudan ladder method that passes its order test on the three cases it was developed on, SA-CCR, and FRTB backtesting zones and desk limits. Also built but not running: a daily paper ledger and a daily curve run. The R6 restriction on the Bermudan ladder stays for both methods (KR-113): the new method `CN_RANNACHER_SMOOTHED` is selectable, the default method is unchanged (`G18_IMPLICIT`), and neither ladder is for hedging. No performance claim is made from paper trading.

## Limits

The stage K mandatory limits text (plan section 15.2) goes here word for word: **[coordinator: paste plan 15.2]**. Limits found in round 1:
