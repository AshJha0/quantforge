## What this release is

The first stage K pieces: a bitemporal store, an incremental curve update, recalibration triggers and intraday risk with an error bound (in Python and Java, with C++ and Rust **[coordinator: confirm from the k-w1 handover at merge time]**), a fixed Bermudan ladder method that passes its order test, SA-CCR and FRTB backtesting zones in Python, and a daily paper ledger that is built but not yet running. It does not lift the R6 restriction on the Bermudan ladder, does not change any default, and makes no performance claim from paper trading.

## Limits

The stage K mandatory limits text (plan section 15.2) goes here word for word: **[coordinator: paste plan 15.2]**. Limits found in round 1:

- Simulated: every paper-ledger fill and every intraday-risk input. Paper ledger rows: "paper marks on a fitted curve; not tradable"; pre-trade estimate on placeholder weights. Paper trading on free data; simulated fills; no evidence of tradable profitability.
- The bitemporal store reads a log passed in the request; there is no persistent store.
- Intraday bounds cover zero-coupon positions only.
- SA-CCR scope (KR-39): no multiple or shared margin agreements, no haircut computation, no central counterparty floors.
- FRTB backtesting covers zones and desk limits only; no P&L attribution test and no capital (KR-47).
- SIMM is not in this release; when built it uses placeholder weights, not ISDA-calibrated.
