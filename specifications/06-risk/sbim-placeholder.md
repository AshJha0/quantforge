The owner ruled on 2026-11-19 that the project uses no ISDA material, because the ISDA SIMM methodology is licensed for a market participant's own transactions only and is covered by a patent; "SIMM" is not a named, delivered item of v1.0.0. **Label rule (KR-130).** Every output carries `label`: "placeholder weights; project-defined sensitivity-based initial margin; not a regulatory or industry-standard calculation". Every doc, table, golden, packet and figure that shows a number from this operation says "placeholder weights" next to it. The placeholder table holds artificial round numbers generated by a stated rule, and its header says so.

**Plain idea.** Two parties that trade uncleared derivatives can post initial margin to each other: collateral sized to cover what the portfolio could lose over the few days it takes to close it out after a default. A sensitivity-based margin model does not simulate anything. It takes the portfolio's sensitivities (how much it gains or loses per small move of each rate, price or volatility), multiplies each by a risk weight, and adds them up with correlations, level by level: risk factors into buckets, buckets into a risk class, risk classes into a group, groups into one number. Large one-way positions get a concentration factor. Option books add a vega charge and a curvature charge for the part of the risk that first-order sensitivities miss. This page defines the project's own aggregation of that kind, for a small, completely specified scope. The weights, correlations and thresholds are artificial, so the output is a check of our own stated algebra, never a margin number.

## Scope (KR-131)

In scope, completely specified:

| Level | In |
|---|---|
| Groups | caller-chosen labels (pattern `[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{0,31}`); nothing nets or diversifies across groups; any in-scope risk class may appear under any group |
| Risk classes | `IR` (interest rate, one curve per currency), `FX`, `EQ` (equity) |
| Margin kinds | IR: delta, vega, curvature. EQ: delta, vega, curvature. FX: delta only |
| Buckets | IR: one bucket per currency. FX: one bucket, key `FX`. EQ: buckets `1`, `2`, `3` and `RESIDUAL` |
| Concentration | delta and vega concentration factors; curvature has none |
| Correlations | within a bucket (tenor matrix for IR, one number per bucket for EQ, one number for FX), across buckets (IR with the `g_bc` concentration ratio, EQ without), across risk classes within a group (`rho_class`) |

Left out (KR-131), each a deliberate cut to keep the scope completely specified: credit and commodity risk classes; FX vega and FX curvature; IR sub-curves, inflation and cross-currency basis risk factors; volatility groups of currencies; any scaling of curvature beyond `SF`; add-ons and multipliers; posting versus collecting directions and regulatory schedules; FX conversion (every amount is already in the calculation currency); any weight that depends on currency or issuer beyond the placeholder rules.

## Formal statement

Notation: `rc` a risk class, `b` a bucket, `k`, `l` risk factors, `s_k` a net delta amount, `VR_k` a net vega amount, `RW`, `VRW` risk weights, `T`, `VT` thresholds, `rho`, `gamma`, `rho_class` correlations, all from the placeholder table. `sqrt+(x)` means `sqrt(max(0, x))` (KR-134). `clip(x, K) = max(-K, min(x, K))`.

The tenor days are those of the placeholder table: `1W` 7, `1Y` 365, `5Y` 1825, `10Y` 3650.

### Step 1: risk factors and netting (F1, KR-132)

Records are grouped by `group` first; nothing nets across groups. Within one group, a record belongs to the risk factor:

| Risk type | Risk factor key | Bucket | Concentration group |
|---|---|---|---|
| `IR_DELTA` | `name + "|" + tenor`, for example `USD|5Y` | `name` (the currency) | the currency |
| `IR_VEGA` | `name + "|" + tenor` (currency and expiry) | the currency | the currency |
| `FX_DELTA` | `name` | `FX` | the currency |
| `EQ_DELTA` | `name` (issuer) | `bucket` | the issuer |
| `EQ_VEGA` | `name` (issuer; all expiries of one issuer are one factor) | `bucket` | the issuer |

Delta and vega are kept apart (a delta factor and a vega factor never net). The net amount of a factor is the sum of its records' amounts: `s_k` for delta, `VR_k` for vega.

### Step 2: concentration (F2)

For each concentration group `g` of a risk class and kind, with `T` the delta threshold (IR 100, FX 1000, EQ 100) or `VT` the vega threshold (IR 1000, EQ 1000) of the table:

`CR_g = max(1, sqrt(abs(sum over the factors k of group g of s_k) / T))` (vega: `VR_k` and `VT`).

The sum is signed before the absolute value: offsetting factors of one group reduce concentration. For IR the group is the whole currency, so every factor of one IR bucket has the same `CR`; that common value is also the bucket's `CR_b` used in step 5. `CR_g = 1` exactly when the absolute sum is at most the threshold.

### Step 3: weighted sensitivity (F3, F7)

Delta: `WS_k = RW_k s_k CR_g(k)`, with `RW_k` the IR weight of the factor's tenor, the FX weight (one number), or the EQ weight of the factor's bucket (the `RESIDUAL` weight for residual factors).
Vega: `WS_k = VRW_rc VR_k CR_g(k)` with the vega weight of the risk class (IR 2, EQ 3) and the vega concentration factor.

### Step 4: within a bucket (F4)

For each non-residual bucket `b`, and separately for the `RESIDUAL` bucket of EQ:

`K_b = sqrt+( sum_k WS_k^2 + sum_{k != l} rho_kl f_kl WS_k WS_l )`, the double sum over ordered pairs (each unordered pair counted twice), with
- `rho_kl`: IR the tenor matrix entry for the two factors' tenors (vega uses the same matrix on the two expiries); FX the single FX correlation; EQ the within-bucket correlation of bucket `b` (`RESIDUAL` has its own), for delta and for vega alike, between any two different issuers of the bucket;
- `f_kl = min(CR_g(k), CR_g(l)) / max(CR_g(k), CR_g(l))` with the delta or vega concentration factors of step 2 as the kind requires (equal to 1 for IR, where the two factors share one group).

`sum_ws_b = sum_{k in b} WS_k`; `S_b = clip(sum_ws_b, K_b)` (KR-133). A kind with a single non-residual bucket has margin `M = K_b` (step 5 with no pair).

### Step 5: across buckets, and the residual bucket (F5, F6)

Over the non-residual buckets of one risk class and kind:

`M = sqrt+( sum_b K_b^2 + sum_{b != c} gamma_bc g_bc S_b S_c )` (ordered pairs), with
- `gamma_bc`: IR the single inter-currency correlation; EQ the single inter-bucket correlation; FX has one bucket and no pair;
- `g_bc = min(CR_b, CR_c) / max(CR_b, CR_c)` for IR (with `CR_b` the bucket's common concentration factor of step 2: the delta factor for delta, the vega factor for vega); `g_bc = 1` for EQ.

The margin of the kind is `margin = M + K_residual`, where `K_residual` is the `K_b` of step 4 for the EQ `RESIDUAL` bucket (0 when there is none). The residual bucket takes part in no inter-bucket sum. When the kind has only residual factors, `M = 0`.

### Step 6: curvature (F8)

Curvature is computed from the vega records of IR and EQ, with no risk weight and no concentration factor.

- Scaling: for a vega record with expiry `tenor` of `t` days, `SF(t) = a min(1, d / t)` with `a` = `sf_scale` (0.25) and `d` = `sf_days` (20) of the table.
- `CVR_k = sum over the vega records of factor k of SF(t) x amount`. Factors are the vega factors of step 1 (IR: currency and expiry; EQ: issuer, summed over its expiries).
- Within a bucket: `K_b = sqrt+( sum_k CVR_k^2 + sum_{k != l} rho_kl^2 CVR_k CVR_l )` with the vega `rho_kl` of step 4 squared and no `f_kl`. `sum_cvr_b = sum_{k in b} CVR_k`, `S_b = clip(sum_cvr_b, K_b)`.
- Across non-residual buckets: `M_c = sqrt+( sum_b K_b^2 + sum_{b != c} gamma_bc^2 S_b S_c )` with no `g_bc`.
- Non-residual: `sum_cvr = sum of CVR_k over the non-residual factors`; `theta = min(sum_cvr / sum of abs(CVR_k), 0)`, and `theta = 0` when that denominator is 0 (KR-135); `lambda = (z^2 - 1)(1 + theta) - theta` with `z` = 2 from the table (so `lambda = 3 + 2 theta`); `C_non = max(sum_cvr + lambda M_c, 0)`.
- Residual (EQ only), computed the same way on the residual factors alone, with their own `sum_cvr_res`, `theta_res`, `lambda_res`, `K_res` (the residual bucket's `K_b`): `C_res = max(sum_cvr_res + lambda_res K_res, 0)`.
- Curvature margin `= C_non + C_res` (a part with no factors is 0).

### Step 7: risk class (F9)

`IM_rc = delta margin + vega margin + curvature margin`, a kind with no records contributing 0.

### Step 8: group (F10)

`IM_g = sqrt+( sum_rc IM_rc^2 + sum_{r != s} rho_class IM_r IM_s )` over the risk classes present in the group (ordered pairs), `rho_class` = 0.2 for every pair.

### Step 9: total (F11)

`total = sum over groups of IM_g`. With no records, `total = 0` and `groups` is empty (KR-136).

### Outputs

All Reals are binary64 JSON numbers; no rounding.

| Path | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `label` | the KR-130 string |
| `total` | step 9 |
| `groups[<g>].im` | step 8; only groups with at least one record |
| `groups[<g>].risk_classes[<rc>].im` | step 7; only risk classes with at least one record in that group |
| `...risk_classes[<rc>].delta`, `.vega` | present only when the kind has records: `margin` (step 5), `buckets[<b>]` {`k_b`, `s_b`, `sum_ws`} for each non-residual bucket with factors, `residual_k` (only when the EQ `RESIDUAL` bucket has factors), `cr[<group>]` (step 2, every concentration group), `ws[<factor key>]` (step 3, every factor) |
| `...risk_classes[<rc>].curvature` | present when `vega` is: `margin` (step 6 total), `cvr[<vega factor key>]`, `buckets[<b>]` {`k_b`, `s_b`, `sum_cvr`} for each non-residual bucket, `sum_cvr`, `theta`, `lambda` (only when non-residual factors exist), `residual` {`k`, `sum_cvr`, `theta`, `lambda`, `margin`} (only when residual factors exist) |

Keys: groups by their labels; risk classes by their enum names; IR buckets by currency, the FX bucket `FX`, EQ buckets `1`, `2`, `3`; factor keys as in step 1; concentration groups by currency (IR, FX) or issuer (EQ). Output order of keys is free.

## Worked example (sbim_k-0008)

USD 5Y delta 400 and EUR 5Y delta 900, both under group `RATES`. Concentration: USD `sqrt(400/100) = 2`, EUR `sqrt(900/100) = 3`. Weighted: USD `30 x 400 x 2 = 24,000`, EUR `30 x 900 x 3 = 81,000`. Each bucket has one factor, so `K_b = abs(WS)` and `S_b = WS`. Across currencies: `gamma g = 0.25 x 2/3 = 1/6`, so `M^2 = 24,000^2 + 81,000^2 + 2 x (1/6) x 24,000 x 81,000 = 576e6 + 6,561e6 + 648e6 = 7,785e6`, `M = 88,232.64701911646`. One risk class, so the group and the total are the same number (placeholder weights).

## Errors (order KR-139 = KR-67 = HR-122)

`E_SCHEMA`: missing `calculation_currency` or `records`; a record that is not an object; a missing or mistyped key; `group` outside its pattern; `risk_type`, `bucket` or `tenor` outside its enum; a currency that is not three upper-case letters; an unknown key in a record. `E_LIMIT`: more than 100,000 records.
