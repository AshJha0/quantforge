# Simulated lit matching engine

Spec version 0.1.0 (stage H wave 1b; confirmed 2026-11-18 by the stage H coordinator). Status: implemented in all four builds (Python, Java, C++, Rust; 2026-11-18). Rulings HR-25 to HR-31. Plan items 15.1 "Lit book (simulated)", 14.7 H-4 (method tests on a simulated book with known truth). **Every output is labelled "simulated venue; not a real exchange".** No real venue's rule book is reproduced; the rules below are a small, stated subset chosen to match common published practice.

**Plain idea.** Orders rest at their price; at one price, the first to arrive is filled first. An incoming order trades against the best opposite prices while it is marketable, then rests, is cancelled, or is rejected according to its type.

## Model
- Integer ticks and integer lots (HR-20); one instrument; prices `tick_size` apart.
- Book: per side, price levels; per level a FIFO queue of orders (MBO). Each order has `id`, `side`, `price`, `qty_open`, `display_qty`, `hidden_qty`, `seq` (arrival counter; the priority key).
- Time is a logical sequence; the engine is deterministic: same input list, same output, in every language.

## Order types (HR-25)
| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| `LIMIT` GTC | match while marketable at resting prices (price improvement to the incoming order), rest the remainder |
| `MARKET` | match against the opposite side to exhaustion of the order or the book; never rests; the unfilled rest is cancelled and reported (`E_` none) |
| `IOC` | limit price; match what is marketable, cancel the rest |
| `FOK` | limit price; pre-check that marketable quantity >= order quantity (including hidden iceberg quantity, HR-27); if yes match in full, else reject with nothing traded |
| `ICEBERG` | `display_qty` shown at the back of the queue; when the shown part is filled, a new slice of `min(display_qty, hidden)` joins the **back** of the queue with a new `seq` ([CME-FIFO] refresh rule); hidden quantity is matchable by an incoming order only after all displayed quantity at that price (HR-27) |
| `POST_ONLY` | if it would trade on arrival it is **rejected** (HR-26; reprice-to-passive is not offered in wave 1b) |
| `PEG_MID` | non-displayed; price = mid of the engine's own best bid and ask, rounded away from crossing (bid down, ask up to a tick, HR-28), capped by its limit; repriced after each event that moves the mid, keeping `seq` while its price is unchanged and taking a new `seq` when it moves; no mid when a side is empty: it does not trade |
| `PEG_PRIMARY` | displayed, priced at the same-side best (excluding itself), capped by its limit, repriced like `PEG_MID`; same `seq` rule |

Modify: a quantity decrease keeps `seq`; a price change or increase takes a new `seq` ([CME-FIFO]). Cancel removes the order.

## Matching (normative)
Incoming marketable order against the opposite side: best price first; within a price, displayed orders by `seq`, then pegged non-displayed orders by `seq`, then iceberg hidden quantity by the iceberg's `seq` (HR-27). Trade price = resting order's price. Self-trade: not checked in wave 1b (one trader id per simulation), stated.

**Peg spacing (ruling, 2026-11-18, the Python lane's rule adopted for all four languages).** After a peg's price is set (mid rounded away from crossing for `PEG_MID`, same-side best for `PEG_PRIMARY`, then capped by its limit), if it would equal or cross the opposite best, where the opposite best counts **every** resting order on that side with a price, other pegs included but not the peg itself, the peg is placed one tick away: a buy peg at opposite best minus one tick, a sell peg at opposite best plus one tick; a result at or below zero leaves the peg unpriced. Reason: a peg never takes liquidity (it is repriced, not matched, by the event that moves it), so it must never lock or cross the book. HR-28's rounding alone does not ensure that: with an even number of ticks between the best bid and ask the mid is on the grid, and two `PEG_MID` orders on opposite sides would both price at the mid and lock the book, breaking the "book never crossed" invariant and making the output depend on which peg reprices first. Stepping one tick away from the opposite best, pegs included, keeps the book uncrossed and unlocked and makes the result independent of language. Java, C++ and Rust implement the same rule (`pegPrice`, `peg_price`).

## Operation `exec.sim.lit.run`
Inputs: `tick_size`, `lot_size`, `events` (list of `{"op": "NEW"|"CANCEL"|"MODIFY", ...}`), `fee_schedule` `{taker_fee, maker_rebate}` per unit notional (signed, HR-30). Outputs: `fills` (`taker_id`, `maker_id`, `price`, `qty`, `seq`), `rejects` (`id`, `reason`: `POST_ONLY_WOULD_CROSS`, `FOK_UNFILLABLE`, `UNKNOWN_ID`), final MBO book and its MBP projection (must equal `micro.book.rebuild` applied to the engine's own level diffs), fees per participant.
Errors: price not on the tick grid, non-positive quantity, a `qty`, `display_qty` or MODIFY quantity that is not a multiple of `lot_size`, MODIFY to 0, `display_qty` > quantity `E_DOMAIN` (HR-128); MODIFY sets the total open quantity, and MODIFY of an id that is not open is the reject `UNKNOWN_ID`; duplicate `id` `E_INPUT`; more than 10,000,000 events `E_LIMIT`; a decimal input or computed value outside the exact range (36 digits, 36 decimals; inputs at most 18 decimals) `E_LIMIT` (HR-57, HR-58).

## Invariants (property tests, every language)
Book never crossed after an event; quantity conserved (sum of fills + resting + cancelled + rejected = submitted); FIFO: for any two fills at one price on displayed quantity, smaller `seq` fills first; FOK fills all or nothing; post-only never takes; replay determinism (byte-identical output).

## Known-truth method tests (H-4)
A Poisson order-flow simulator (`core.rng`, seed fixed per test) in the [CST10] model of `dark-pool.md`'s fill section, with planted arrival, cancel and market-order rates; the estimators of arrival and cancellation rates from the engine's event log recover the planted rates within 3 SE.

## Golden value cases (stage H wave 2, DR-H2 blind lane)

Written 2026-11-18 by the spec lane. One case per order type (LIMIT 0002, FOK 0003, IOC 0004, POST_ONLY 0005, ICEBERG 0006, PEG_MID 0008, MARKET 0009, PEG_PRIMARY 0010), FIFO (0001, 0007) and conservation (0011). Inputs and outputs are canonical JSON (sorted keys, no spaces, HR-29); prices, quantities and fees are decimal strings, fees with no exponent and no trailing zeros ("0" for zero), HR-12.

Harness conventions (fixed before the blind lane starts):
- `NEW` events carry `id`, `trader`, `side` (`BUY`/`SELL`), `type`, `qty`, `price` (the limit; absent for `MARKET`; for pegs, the cap) and `display_qty` for `ICEBERG`. `MODIFY` carries `id` and the new `qty` (open displayed quantity). `seq` is one counter starting at 1, taken by every resting order on arrival and on every new-`seq` event (iceberg refresh, modify up, peg price move); an order that never rests (rejected, fully filled on arrival, IOC, FOK, MARKET) takes none.
- The mid for `PEG_MID` and the same-side best for `PEG_PRIMARY` use resting non-`PEG_MID` orders only; a peg's first price on arrival keeps its arrival `seq`; repricing runs after each event completes, in `seq` order.
- Output object: `fills` (`taker_id`, `maker_id`, `price`, `qty`, `seq` = fill counter from 1), `rejects` (`id`, `reason`), `cancelled` (`id`, `qty`: explicit cancels, unfilled IOC, MARKET remainders), `book` (`bids` by price descending then `seq`, `asks` by price ascending then `seq`; each `{"id", "price", "display_qty", "hidden_qty", "seq"}`; a `PEG_MID` shows `display_qty` "0" and its quantity as `hidden_qty`), `mbp` (`[price, displayed qty]` per non-empty level), `fees` per trader (`taker_fee` times notional charged, `maker_rebate` times notional paid as negative; a trader appears once it has a fill), `conservation` per order id (`submitted` = original qty plus modify increases; `filled` as taker or maker; `resting`; `cancelled` including modify decreases; `rejected`), and `label` "simulated venue; not a real exchange".
- The book-crossing and FOK checks count hidden iceberg and non-displayed peg quantity (HR-27).
