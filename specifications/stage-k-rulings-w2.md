# Stage K rulings, wave 2 regulatory capital (K-8a, K-8f, K-8g, K-8i): PROPOSED

Written 2026-11-19 by the stage K wave 2 spec lane (reading and writing only; no code, no runs). Status: **PROPOSED** until the coordinator confirms. Numbering starts at KR-50 so it cannot collide with wave 1 (KR-1 to KR-11 on `k-spec`). Append-only. Source of record for every number: the Basel Framework PDF generated 11/11/2026, read 2026-11-19 (`program/STAGE_K_SOURCES_W2.md`); parameter tables in `specifications/tables/stage_k/`.

Why these rulings exist: the Basel text leaves choices open that four language builds must make the same way (sign conventions, tie rules, boundary equality, what happens under a square root that goes negative), and in two places the formula image and the paragraph text differ. Each ruling says which way we go and why; none changes a published number.

| KR-53 | Offsetting within an obligor | Seniority ranks: `COVERED` 4, `SENIOR` 3, `NON_SENIOR` 2, `EQUITY` 1. A short may offset a long of equal or higher rank (MAR22.19(1)). Deterministic greedy rule: take shorts in descending rank (ties: input order); each consumes eligible longs in ascending rank (ties: input order) until it is used up. A short of rank r may use the longs of rank r or above, so the eligible sets are nested (a junior short's set contains a senior short's set); serving the most restricted shorts first therefore offsets the largest total possible, and since one obligor has one rating (KR-51) the net amounts do not depend on which eligible long was used. Unmatched longs sum to the obligor's net long, unmatched shorts to its net short; an obligor can have both (MAR22.21). Ruling on `COVERED`: MAR22 gives covered bonds an LGD, not a seniority; we rank them as the most senior debt. |

Spearman per MAR32.38 on the ranks (Pearson correlation of ranks, population covariance and standard deviations; the n or n-1 factor cancels). A constant series has zero rank variance: `E_DOMAIN`. | KR-327 | A3 of `frtb-drc-rrao.md`: "adding a long only to a bucket never lowers that bucket's DRC" is false; the spec is wrong, not the property test | **Plain idea.** DRC_b = max(WtdLong - HBR x WtdShort, 0) with HBR = (sum of net longs) / (sum of net longs + sum of absolute net shorts) (MAR22.24-22.25). A new long raises the HBR, which raises the charge on the shorts, and it can also offset the same obligor's short, which lowers the net short sum and raises the HBR further. When its own risk weight is small next to the shorts', DRC_b falls. **Formal.** For a long added to an obligor without a net short, with risk weight r, net long sum L, net short sum S, T = L + S, weighted short sum WS: d(WtdLong - HBR x WS)/dL = r - WS x S / T^2, negative whenever r < WS x S / T^2 while DRC_b > 0. **Worked example (measured 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20).** The book pinned as `FrtbDrcTest.a3MonotonicityCounterexample` (Java, found by the jqwik property at seed -24) and `test_drc_a3_monotonicity_counterexample` (Python reference): CORPORATE DRC_b 18.67915216395695 before and 17.739318324443047 after adding a covered long of 885 to obligor A (HBR 0.288 to 0.395; here the long offsets A's shorts). Python and Java agree. A random search with the Python reference (100,000 books of 1 to 6 obligors, every rating, both buckets; seed 2) found the A3 sentence false for 1,384 of those books. **Proposed replacement for the A3 sentence:** "adding a long to an obligor that has no net short, whose risk weight is at least the risk weight of every obligor with a net short in the same bucket, never lowers that bucket's DRC". Proof: such an obligor's shorts are already fully offset, the KR-53 greedy offset is maximal, so the extra long cannot create a net short; S and WS are unchanged and WS <= r_max x S <= r x S, so the derivative above is at least r - r x S^2 / T^2 >= 0 along the whole addition, and the floor at 0 keeps the order. The same search met the premise for 55,323 books and found 0 violations (measured, 2026-11-20). **Tests.** Python `test_drc_properties` and Java `FrtbDrcTest.drcProperties` (now 10,000 tries, the A3 count) assert the corrected statement; the false sentence is not asserted anywhere; the pinned counterexample stays in both builds with this explanation. No golden changes. The spec sentence itself is left as written until the coordinator confirms this ruling; then A3 of `frtb-drc-rrao.md` is replaced by the sentence above. |
