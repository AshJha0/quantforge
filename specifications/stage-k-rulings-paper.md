| KR-200 | Recorded trading day (amends KR-82 and KR-87; audit KA-C2, KA-C12); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | The cut-off does not move: a row for business day D is on time before 19:30:00 UTC on the next `USGS` business day, the earliest instant at which an input for that next day can exist (the 3:30 PM Eastern quotes, in summer time). What changes is the evidence. A row's `timing` label and its `ts` are the writing run's own clock. They decide whether the rule sends an order, they are checked against each other, and they are reported as `declared_on_time_days`; they are never the K-2 count. A **recorded trading day** is a DAY row for D that is labelled on time, in a ledger that passes the whole verification (chain, replay, rebuild, calendar, and `SOURCE` of KR-211, which ties the row to the curve published for D and to no other), and for which an anchor covering the row carries a Bitcoin block time T with T + 7,200 s before D's cut-off (KR-201). A row with no such anchor is not counted, whatever it says. An honest day counts: by the measurements of KR-206 its row is written by about 05:15 UTC and its block follows within 1.6 h. A day written, or first stamped, after the next day's inputs could exist cannot count, because no block time before the cut-off covers it |
| KR-201 | Anchor evidence (both daily jobs); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | An anchor is the file `{count, head, ...}` stamped with OpenTimestamps. Its time is read from the proof, not from the file's name, its `time` field or a git commit. `tools/paper_daily/anchor_evidence.py` (standard library, no network) recomputes the proof's operations from the SHA-256 of the anchor file to a Merkle root; requires that root to be bytes 36 to 68 of an 80-byte Bitcoin block header supplied by the person verifying; requires the header's double SHA-256 to meet the header's own target; and requires that target to be at or below 2^180 (a difficulty of about 1.8e13). Measured 2026-11-19 on the 48 headers named by this repository's forward-log anchors (blocks 975226 to 976511): targets near 2^177, difficulty 1.35e14, so the floor is about one eighth of today's difficulty and far above the easiest target the header format allows. The block time is bytes 68 to 72. Only SHA-256, append and prepend are recomputed; the format's other operations (SHA-1, RIPEMD-160, Keccak-256, reverse, hexlify) are passed through and what lies under one is no evidence; a tag that is not an operation is an error. A pending proof, a missing proof and a header that was not supplied are no evidence (the day is not counted). A proof or header that does not match is an error (verification fails). Margin: 7,200 s is taken off every bound, because the miner writes the block time and consensus bounds it only loosely (above the median of the previous eleven blocks; nodes refuse more than two hours ahead). Not checked: that the header is on the best chain at the stated height; the proof-of-work floor stands in for that, and the limit is stated in the spec. A git commit time, signed or not, is the committer's own clock and is not used as evidence |
| KR-202 | The K-2 gate command (amends KR-87; audit KA-C3); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | `verify_ledger.py verify --ledger L --anchors A --forward-log F --bitcoin-headers H --require-days 20`. `--require-days` above 0 refuses with reason `GATE` under `--no-cli` or without any one of the three other inputs. `verify_ledger.py heights --anchors A` lists the block heights whose headers `H` must hold and the anchors still pending; any Bitcoin node or public block explorer serves a header by height, and a wrong one fails by KR-201. Run `ots upgrade` on the proofs first (the workflow also does so on each run). Without `--bitcoin-headers` the verifier reports `recorded_trading_days` as null: the label alone is never printed as the count |
| KR-205 | A run with nothing due writes nothing (both jobs; audit KA-C1); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | No row, no commit on the ledger branch, no new anchor. The paper job's verification summary goes to the runner's temporary folder, never to the branch. The push step stages named files only. The anchor step makes an anchor only when none is held for exactly the current `(count, head)`: a head is stamped once, and a head whose stamp failed is stamped by the next run. The anchors branch also gets a commit when `ots upgrade` adds a Bitcoin attestation to an older proof (a few per proof at most). An anchor file is never overwritten |
| KR-206 | When the jobs run (both jobs; audit KA-C12); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | Measured 2026-11-19 from GitHub's own start times (`gh run list`) of the 24 scheduled runs of `recorder.yml` and `daily-workflows.yml` from 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19: the 23:30 UTC slot started 3.41 to 5.67 h after its cron time (n = 8, median 4.68 h); the 14:30 UTC slot 6.44 to 9.00 h (n = 8, median 7.37 h); the 15:00 UTC slot 6.24 to 8.73 h (n = 8). No scheduled run was dropped. The audited "second chance" slots (15:10 and 14:15 UTC) would therefore have started after 19:30 UTC on every one of those days, and the audited 00:40 UTC slot could start before the recorder had written the day's curve. The triggers of both jobs are withheld on the integration branch (commit 1d184366, 2026-11-19) and stay comments in the workflow files until these rulings are confirmed and CI job `python-daily-jobs-bytes` is green on the release commit. The triggers to restore are: for the paper ledger, the completion of the recorder workflow (`workflow_run`), with cron slots at 03:00 and 06:00 UTC, Tuesday to Saturday, as second and third chances; for the curve run, cron slots at 23:45 UTC Monday to Friday and at 03:30 and 06:30 UTC Tuesday to Saturday (a third slot, because its old second one could only produce late rows). Ten days of measurements are not a guarantee: a day whose row or block is later than KR-200 allows is not counted, and the verifier names it |
| KR-208 | Canonical bytes (both jobs); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | A ledger file is ASCII lines and nothing else: each line is the row's JSON with keys sorted by code point at every level, separators `,` and `:`, non-ASCII characters escaped, ended by one line feed. The verifier fails (`BYTES`) any other serialisation of the same rows (carriage returns, spaces, another key order, a missing final line feed), so the file's bytes follow from its rows. Files are read and written as bytes, and the ledger branches carry `* -text`. A row time is exactly `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`. `tools/paper_daily/canonical_selftest.py` pins the bytes and SHA-256 of fixed rows, the number strings, and the genesis row of each real configuration. CI job `python-daily-jobs-bytes` runs it on `ubuntu-24.04`, under pytest and under the runner's own `python3` (the interpreter the scheduled jobs use), together with the paper ledger's golden hashes. The first scheduled run waits for that job to be green on the release commit. Numbers that pass through `exp`, `log` or `pow` are not canonical across platforms; KR-86 and KR-207 cover them |
| KR-210 | No state stops the paper ledger (audit KA-C9, applied to this writer); status: CONFIRMED 2026-11-21 by the stage K coordinator, delegated by owner | (a) A due date inside a prereg holdout window gets a GAP row with `cause` "holdout", the window ids in `studies`, and no input read. The writer no longer refuses: a refusal would repeat on every later run and stop the ledger for good. A DAY row dated inside a window still fails verification, and the ledger never starts on a holdout date. (b) Every GAP row carries `cause`: `no_curve`, `holdout` or `build_refused`. (c) If a day's row cannot be built, or does not replay, then before that day's cut-off the run stops at the day, keeps the rows before it, prints `PROBLEM:` and exits 2; after the cut-off the day is closed with a `build_refused` gap and the later days are written. (f) `run_day.py` exits 0 when done, 1 when it refused, 2 when rows were written and one is a GAP or a late day, or a day could not be built (the workflow pushes the rows and then turns the run red), as the curve run's KR-107.
