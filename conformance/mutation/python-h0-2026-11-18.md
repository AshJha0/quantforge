## roots: why the run was slow, and what to do next time

The roots target uses suite `*`, so every sampled mutant runs the whole golden set, and the
root-finder loops are the place where a mutant (a bracket update swapped, `<=` to `<` on the stop
test, `max_iter + 1` changes) can make every solver call in every golden spin to `max_iter` or
loop without end. `Golden` allows 900 s per mutant, so a few such mutants alone take hours.
Before rerunning: lower the golden limit for roots (for example 120 s, about 3x the unmutated
golden run time, measured first with `golden.baseline`), or score roots with a narrower suite
(the root-finder and rulings goldens) and run the full `*` suite only on survivors.
