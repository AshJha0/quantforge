## Key formulas
Swap: `F = sum (P(s_j)/P(e_j) - 1) D(e_j)`. Tenor basis: `sum (P(s)/P(e) - D(s)/D(e)) D(e) / sum alpha D(e)`.

The specs hold the full definitions and edge cases.
