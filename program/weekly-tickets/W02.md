# W02: Dates, calendars and conventions

Stage A. A rate means nothing until its day count, calendar and compounding are stated.

## Specification
`01-core/daycount.md`, `calendars.md`, `compounding.md`, `timezone-and-cutoff.md`. Paths are under `specifications/` unless stated.

## Key formulas
`ACT_360 = days / 360`. `30/360 = (360 dY + 30 dM + dD) / 360` after day adjustment.

ACT/ACT ISDA across years: `(Jan1(y1+1) - start)/D(y1) + (y2 - y1 - 1) + (end - Jan1(y2))/D(y2)`.

Discount factors: `1/(1 + r t)`, `(1 + r/n)^(-n t)`, `exp(-r t)`.

The specs hold the full definitions and edge cases.
