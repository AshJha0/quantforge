Synthetic data. Continuous rates, `F = S exp((r_quote - r_base) t)`. Values from `oracle/python/gen_exercises.py`.

| 1.28 | 0.04 | 0.04 | 1.0 | 1.28000000 | 0.00000000 |
| 150.0 | 0.045 | 0.005 | 0.5 | 153.03020100 | 3.03020100 |

## Answers

1. Quoting USDGBP in place of GBPUSD inverts the rate, and swaps which curve is on top. Name base and quote in the type.
2. With equal rates the forward equals spot, as in row 2.
3. As a signed number of pips, with `pip` from the pair's record: `(F - S)/pip`. Never as a bare difference.

Explanation: `specifications/02-curves/fx.md`.
