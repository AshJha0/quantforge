## Answers

1. Here DV01 is the change in PV for rates up 1 bp, signed. A payer gains, so it is positive.
2. Too large a bump picks up convexity; too small a bump drowns in solver and rounding noise.
3. Parallel moves all quotes at once. Key-rate moves one, showing where on the curve the risk sits. They sum to parallel to first order only.
