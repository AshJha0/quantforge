# Exercise: Implied volatility

## Task
Invert a Black-style option price to implied volatility with bounds and convergence reporting.

## Questions
1. What are no-arbitrage price bounds?
1. How do you handle expiry?
1. Why is a robust bracket useful?

## Submission requirements
- Code and tests in the relevant language directory.
- A short mathematical explanation.
- Normal, boundary and invalid cases.
- Expected output and independent verification method.
- Complexity/performance note where relevant.
