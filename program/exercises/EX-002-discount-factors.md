# Exercise: Discount factors

## Task
Implement continuous and periodic compounding conversions and discount factors. Verify limiting cases and inverse identities.

## Questions
1. How does a negative rate affect a discount factor?
1. What units should rates use?
1. How do you avoid percentage/basis-point ambiguity?

## Submission requirements
- Code and tests in the relevant language directory.
- A short mathematical explanation.
- Normal, boundary and invalid cases.
- Expected output and independent verification method.
- Complexity/performance note where relevant.
