# Implementation programme

- `weekly-tickets/`: 36 tickets, W01 to W36. W01 to W05 are stage A and hold formulas, suites and four-language tasks. W06 to W36 are still starter briefs, regenerated before their stage.
- `exercises/`: 12 exercises. EX-001 to EX-003 have data and solutions; the rest are questions only until their stage.
- `traceability.md`: every blueprint item mapped to a stage or a stated exclusion.
- `definition-of-done.md`: the common quality bar.

The tickets are planning briefs. Stage A's boxes are not ticked in the files; `CHANGELOG.md` and the conformance run record what was done. A ticket may be split into smaller issues if that makes it easier to review.
