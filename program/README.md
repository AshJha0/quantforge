# Implementation programme

- `weekly-tickets/`: 36 tickets, W01 to W36. W01 to W05 are stage A, and W06 to W12, W20 and W25 are stage B; these hold formulas, suites and four-language tasks. W07, W20 and W25 cover only their stage B parts. The other tickets are still starter briefs, regenerated before their stage. Stage B tickets name the first suites; the `_deep` suites and `rulings_b` are in `specifications/stage-b-followups.md`.
- `exercises/`: 12 exercises. EX-001 to EX-003, EX-005, EX-006, EX-008 and EX-009 have data and solutions (EX-008 reuses the EX-006 quotes); the rest are questions only until their stage.
- `traceability.md`: every blueprint item mapped to a stage or a stated exclusion.
- `definition-of-done.md`: the common quality bar.

The tickets are planning briefs. The boxes of stages A and B are not ticked in the files; `CHANGELOG.md` and the conformance run record what was done. A ticket may be split into smaller issues if that makes it easier to review.
