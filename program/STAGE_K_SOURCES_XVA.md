## Findings from reading (recorded for the coordinator)

1. **LS01 printed values are reproducible.** The eight-path example (pp. 115-119) gives 0.1144 (American) and 0.0564 (European); a scratch re-implementation of the printed algorithm gives 0.11443433 and 0.05638074, and regression coefficients (-1.06999, 2.98341, -1.81358) at time 2 and (2.03751, -3.33544, 1.35646) at time 1 against the printed (-1.070, 2.983, -1.813) and (2.038, -3.335, 1.356). The printed continuation values at time 1 (0.0139, 0.1092, 0.2866, 0.1175, 0.1533) are evaluated from the *rounded* coefficients; full-precision coefficients give 0.01349, 0.10875, 0.28606, 0.11701, 0.15276. The exercise decisions are the same either way. Measured 2026-11-19.
2. **LS01 Table 1 "finite difference" column is the 50-dates-a-year Bermudan value**, not the continuously exercisable American: QuantLib 1.44 `FdBlackScholesVanillaEngine` with 50 exercise dates a year gives 4.47778, 2.31404, 1.10985, 5.31193, 4.84018, 3.94766, 2.88452 for the rows (36, .20, 1), (40, .20, 1), (44, .20, 1), (40, .40, 1), (36, .20, 2), (44, .40, 1), (40, .20, 2), matching the printed 4.478, 2.314, 1.110, 5.312, 4.840, 3.948, 2.885; the American values (4.48644, ...) do not. Measured 2026-11-19 (grid 2000 x 800; a 4000 x 1600 grid moves each value by less than 2e-5).
3. **Licence wording conflict in the repo.** `xva-exposure-portfolio.md` and `cva-capital.md` call ORE "Apache 2.0"; the ORE survey read `license.txt` at the tag as Modified BSD, and `oracle/LICENCES.md` and `ore-g42b.yml` say Modified BSD. Not changed here (outside this lane's files); for the docs lane.
4. **ORE survival timing differs between its FVA and MVA formulas** ([ORE-X]): FVA weights by `S_C(t_(i-1)) S_B(t_(i-1))`, MVA by `S_C(t_i) S_B(t_i)`. Our rulings follow each formula as written so that the ORE route compares like with like (KR-164, KR-165).
5. **`dim` is a flag of the `xva` analytic, not an analytic type** ([ORE-P]); the survey's phrase "the `dim` analytic" should read "the `dim` flag of `xva`".
